Personal project

AR gestures

Real-time hand tracking in the browser that maps five gestures to elemental effects anchored to the palm, with no build step and nothing to install.

Overview

An augmented reality demo that runs entirely in the browser from the webcam. MediaPipe Hands tracks 21 landmarks on one hand in real time; the gesture is recognised from those landmarks, and an effect is drawn on a canvas overlay that follows the hand.

GestureEffect
Open palmWind: particles orbiting the palm on three elliptical rings
FistEarth: rotating rocks and falling dust
Index finger upLightning: branching bolts from the fingertip
PinchIce: orbiting snowflakes and expanding ripples
Peace signFire: rising flame particles

Recognising gestures

  • A finger counts as raised when its tip is above its middle joint. Pinch is checked first, as a small distance between the thumb tip and the index tip.
  • The other gestures follow from which fingers are raised: all four for an open palm, index and middle for peace, only the index for pointing, none for a fist.
  • To avoid flicker, the label is a majority vote over the last five frames, and a gesture has to hold for 300 ms before its effect starts.

Rendering

  • Effects are anchored to the centre of the palm (lightning to the index fingertip) and scale with the size of the hand in the image, so they look the same close to the camera or far away.
  • Each element is a small particle system with its own update and draw step: fire uses short-lived particles with velocity damping, lightning regenerates a recursive midpoint-displacement bolt several times a second, and ice draws its snowflakes procedurally.
  • The frame time step is capped, dead particles are removed every frame and effect objects are reused, which keeps the animation smooth.
