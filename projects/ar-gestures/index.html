Personal project

AR gestures

Real-time hand tracking in the browser that maps five gestures to visual effects anchored to the palm.

Overview

An augmented reality demo that runs entirely in the browser. MediaPipe Hands provides hand landmarks from the webcam in real time, the gesture is recognised from those landmarks, and a particle effect is rendered on a canvas overlay that follows the palm.

GestureEffect
Open palmWind
FistEarth
Index finger upLightning
PinchIce
Peace signFire

A gesture has to be held briefly before its effect starts. There is no build step and nothing to install.

Stack
