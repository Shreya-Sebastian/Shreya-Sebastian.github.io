Social inclusion from sensor data

Measures the social inclusion of preschool children with hearing loss from wearable positioning and audio data. I fused the sensor streams, identified six activity contexts with a Gaussian Mixture Model and compared the two groups of children within each context using mixed-effects models.

BSc thesis · Leiden University

3D CNNs for pollen classification

Classifies nettle-family pollen from microscope Z-stacks using every focal plane. I trained 3D CNNs in PyTorch, and a ResNet3D trained on the full stack reached 96.7% test accuracy with only about 10% of the data used in earlier 2D-projection work.
