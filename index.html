About

I completed my MSc in Computer Science at TU Delft on the Artificial Intelligence Technology track. My thesis, supervised in the Socially Perceptive Computing Lab, used unsupervised learning on fused positioning and audio data to discover activity contexts in an inclusive preschool, and mixed-effects models to compare children with and without hearing loss within each context.

Alongside that I build systems end to end. Tech Jobs Radar is a job board I designed, deployed and run, with crawling, classification, a CI evaluation gate and a Kubernetes deployment on AWS. RetroSTC-Net is a research project in PyTorch on retroactive consolidation in neural networks, inspired by synaptic tagging and capture.

Before TU Delft I took a BSc in Computer Science at Leiden University on the bioinformatics track. I am a native English speaker with conversational Dutch, based in the Netherlands and available immediately. Outside work I play basketball.
